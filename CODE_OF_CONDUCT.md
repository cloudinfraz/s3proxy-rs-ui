# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in `s3proxy-rs-ui` respectful,
constructive, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Give and receive feedback constructively.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Do not share credentials, private logs, or sensitive operational details.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, publication of
private information, secrets, threats, spam, and intentionally misleading
reports are unacceptable.

## Enforcement and reporting

Maintainers may limit participation or report abuse when behavior harms the
community. Report security vulnerabilities through `SECURITY.md`; report conduct
concerns through a private owner-profile channel.
